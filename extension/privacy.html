Crystal GIF PFP

Shared animated pictures for people using the Crystal browser extension. Unaffiliated with Google or YouTube.

Privacy and sharing

Local mode keeps your GIF and selected image address in your Chrome profile. Shared mode is optional. When enabled, visible YouTube channel handles or IDs are sent to this service to find shared pictures. Full video URLs, video titles, comments, and watch history are not sent.

Publishing stores your uploaded GIF, verified public channel ID, handle, title, public avatar image key, timestamps, and a hashed management token. Published GIFs and channel associations are public and may be downloaded. Never upload private or sensitive material.

Verification reads your public channel description through YouTube's API to check a temporary code. Your YouTube password is never requested. Remove the code after verification. Verification must be renewed after 30 days. Expired profiles and their GIFs are removed by daily cleanup.

The service stores a hash of the connecting IP address in short-lived rate-limit buckets until daily cleanup (up to three days). Challenge records expire after one hour. Cloudflare and Google process requests under their own policies. No analytics, advertising, sale of data, or third-party GIF providers are used by this extension.

Control and removal

Use Delete shared profile in the extension to remove your public record and credentials. Cached GIFs can remain for up to five minutes; copies downloaded by others cannot be recalled. Uninstalling removes local settings but does not delete a published profile. Reverify your channel to recover management access.

You can disable shared pictures or block a specific channel locally. Use Report in the extension for abusive GIFs; reports store the channel ID, count, and timestamp for the operator. Reported content is not automatically removed. Only upload images you have permission to share.

Contact the publisher through the extension's Chrome Web Store support contact for privacy requests.
