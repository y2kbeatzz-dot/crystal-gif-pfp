Start here

Three steps. Then make it yours.

  1. Download & extract

    Download the ZIP above. Choose Extract All and keep the extracted folder somewhere permanent.

  2. Add it to Chrome

    Open chrome://extensions. Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select the extension folder inside crystal-gif-pfp-main.

  3. Choose your GIF

    Refresh YouTube. Open Crystal, choose a GIF, then use Select my picture on YouTube to pick your profile picture.

Want others to see it? Publish your GIF from the extension. Other Crystal users only need the extension installed and See other members’ GIFs enabled. They do not need to sign into YouTube.
Good to know

A few quick answers.

Will everyone on YouTube see my GIF?

Only people using Crystal with community pictures enabled can see published GIFs. Your regular YouTube profile picture stays the same for everyone else.

What GIFs can I use?

Local GIFs can be up to 5 MB. Shared GIFs must be real GIF files, 1 MB or smaller, and no larger than 512 × 512 pixels. The extension now checks this for you and includes quick optimizer, resize, and convert tools.

How do I share or remove my picture?

Sign into your YouTube channel, choose your GIF, enter your @handle, and publish. Crystal automatically checks the signed-in account. Use Delete shared profile to remove it; uninstalling alone does not delete a published GIF.

Is this available in the Chrome Web Store?

Not yet. This is a GitHub preview installed through Load unpacked. Community sharing is live on Worker v4, including cross-account viewing by channel reference and wide-sidebar channel layouts.
